Privacy Policy

Draft updated August 29, 2026

What we collect

Legofolio may collect account identifiers such as email address, username, authentication-provider identifiers, collection items, purchase prices, estimated values, watchlist activity, and marketplace account information you choose to connect. We also process basic technical information needed for security and operation, such as authentication records and request metadata.

Marketplace credentials

Connected marketplace API credentials are sent to server-side functions and stored encrypted. They are not intentionally placed in public website code. Use read-only marketplace permissions whenever possible. No online service can promise absolute security.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, display and calculate portfolios, import authorized marketplace listings, provide catalog and pricing features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

Legofolio currently relies on Supabase for authentication, database, and server functions. If enabled, Google and Apple process social login. LEGO catalog and marketplace information may come from providers such as Brickset, Rebrickable, Brick Owl, or eBay under their respective terms. These providers process information under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not state that personal information is sold. Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the app, when you direct a marketplace connection, to protect users and the service, or when legally required. Public listing comparisons must not expose private credentials.

Storage and retention

Account and portfolio information is retained while an account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, backup, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Final retention periods must be established before launch. Browser storage may remember authentication sessions and display preferences.

Your choices

You may disconnect a marketplace account and can request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information. A self-service account deletion mechanism and verified privacy contact must be available before public launch.

Children

Legofolio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. Do not create an account if you are under 13. Age and parental-consent requirements may be higher in some locations.

Security

We use access controls, row-level database policies, encryption for connected marketplace credentials, and restricted server-side secrets. Users remain responsible for protecting their accounts and using unique passwords.

International users and legal rights

Privacy rights vary by location and may include access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection. The operator must determine applicable state, federal, and international requirements before launch.

Contact

Before launch, replace this section with the operator’s legal name and monitored privacy email. During development, contact the repository owner through the project repository.
